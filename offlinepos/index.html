OfflinePOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

OfflinePOS is an offline-first point-of-sale app. It does not require an account or internet connection for core use.

Data collected

  • Sales, products, purchases, and shifts: Stored locally on the device only. Not transmitted unless you explicitly configure an optional admin URL in Settings.
  • Admin PIN: Stored locally (hashed). Never leaves the device.
  • Bluetooth: Used only for optional phone-to-phone sync of catalog/sales and for receipt printers. No continuous scanning or advertising of personal data.
  • Camera: Used only when you choose to scan a barcode. Images are processed on-device and not stored or uploaded.
  • Network: Optional. When a network is available and an admin URL is set, the app may send daily sales totals (date, count, total, profit, device ID). Nothing is sent if the URL is empty.

Data sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties for advertising or tracking. Optional admin-URL posts go only to the URL you provide.

Permissions

  • Camera: barcode scanning
  • Bluetooth: printer and phone-to-phone sync
  • Internet / Network state: optional daily totals upload and barcode lookup (Open Food Facts, cached locally)
  • Foreground service: keep Bluetooth sync alive while linked

Data retention & deletion

All data stays on the device. Uninstalling the app deletes it. You can also clear app data in Android settings.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer via the Play Console listing.

This policy will be updated if data practices change.
